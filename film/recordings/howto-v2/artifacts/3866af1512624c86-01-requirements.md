**Precedence applied:** approved PID > approved BRD > working material *(Program knowledge rules)*. Where the BRD and PID disagree, the PID wins for Release 1 and the conflict is listed for the owner.

## How to read this

- **IDs:** Existing refined IDs (BR-001.1 … BR-008.1) are kept. New items take the next free number under their parent. Nothing is renumbered or deleted.
- **Status:** `Approved` = unchanged existing item. `Draft (AI)` = new. `Draft (AI) – revision` = an existing item whose wording I propose to change because a source changed. `Withdrawn` / `Deferred (R2)` = kept for traceability, not in Release 1.
- **Type:** Functional, Non-functional, Data or Business rule.
- **Open questions** are numbered Q1–Q26 and listed after the table.
