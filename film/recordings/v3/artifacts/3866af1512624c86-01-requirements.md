Key points:
1. **BR-004.1 is out of date.** The TRM still says photos must be taken within **12 months**. BRD v1.3 changed this to **6 months** on 2026-10-28.
2. **SMS conflict.** BRD §3.6 asks for email *and SMS* notifications. The approved PID §3 puts SMS **out of scope for Release 1**. Following the PID, email is in Release 1 and SMS is deferred to Release 2.
3. **BR-008.1 (paper permit) is still Approved** in the TRM, but its parent BR-008 was withdrawn on 2026-10-15 (decision SC-14). It is marked Withdrawn here.
4. **Performance and availability (BRD §4) had no refined requirements.** They are added as BR-006.3 and BR-006.4.
5. **Untestable wording:** "in a timely manner" (BR-005), "the photo standard" (BR-004, not among the program documents) and "renewal window" (BR-001.1, never defined in the BRD).

## 6. Assumptions

- **A1:** "Signed-in" in BR-001.1 means the enterprise sign-in (PID §4). Identity proofing beyond sign-in is not assumed (see NEW-C).
- **A2:** BRD v1.3 is the current approved BRD. Its change history shows v1.2 as the "Approved baseline", and the v1.3 changes are logged by the Business Owner in the TRM Change Log, but no v1.3 approval is recorded. **Please confirm.**
- **A3:** Items marked Approved in the TRM are carried over unchanged. Where they combine several checks (for example BR-003.1: fee, card and ordering), a split is recommended at their next revision but has not been made here.
- **A4:** The receipt email in BR-003.2 is allowed in Release 1 because email notifications are in scope (PID §3).
