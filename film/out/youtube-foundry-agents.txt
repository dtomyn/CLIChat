Lumen + Microsoft Foundry: SDLC agents in one friendly chat

A proof of concept: 23 AI agents for the software delivery lifecycle, built with LangGraph and hosted in Microsoft Foundry. This video shows business users working with them from Lumen: setup in one field, an agent gallery, cited and structured answers from real hosted agents, and human approval before anything changes.
